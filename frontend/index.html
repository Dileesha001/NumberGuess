🏏 How to Play Mini Cricket

  • 1. Start/Bowl: Press the Enter ↵ key to start the match and bowl.
  • 2. Hit: Watch the ball. Press either 5 key (keyboard or Numpad) to bat exactly as the ball crosses the crease.
  • 3. Auto-Bowl: After each play completes, the next delivery will bowl automatically!
  • 4. Ends Change: After every 6 balls (an Over), the bowling direction changes!
